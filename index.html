I’m a first-year Information Technology student at Netaji Subhash Engineering College, Kolkata. My interests sit around local AI, privacy engineering, and the hardware that brings software into everyday life.

I like tracing a system from input to outcome: where the data goes, which assumptions hold it together, and what happens when something fails. These projects are how I’m learning to ask better questions—and test the answers.
